A clear beginning

Care should make sense from day one.

We begin with your history, your current concerns, and the activities you want to return to. Dr. Nguyen then evaluates relevant movement, mobility, and clinical findings before discussing recommendations.

You will have time to ask questions and understand the purpose of any recommended care.

01

Conversation

Tell us what happened, what you are feeling, what changes your symptoms, and what you want to be able to do comfortably.

02

Examination

Dr. Nguyen evaluates posture, range of motion, movement, and relevant orthopedic or neurological findings.

03

Explanation

You receive a straightforward explanation of the findings, whether chiropractic care may be appropriate, and the recommended plan.

04

Treatment

Treatment may begin during the first visit when clinically appropriate and with your consent.

Before you arrive

What to bring

Please allow approximately 60 minutes.Your first visit includes the examination and treatment when appropriate.
  • Comfortable clothing that allows easy movement
  • Photo identification and relevant insurance information
  • Imaging reports or prior records related to your concern
  • Accident details, claim information, or attorney contact when applicable
  • A list of medications and questions you want answered

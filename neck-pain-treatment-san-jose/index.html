A Personalized Approach to Neck Pain

Neck pain is one of the most frequent concerns Dr. Lam Nguyen, D.C. evaluates at VAL Chiropractic in San Jose. It can follow a car accident or sports injury, or build gradually through desk work, screen use, repetitive stress and limited mobility. Your visit begins with a conversation and focused examination so the recommendation is based on your findings—not a one-size-fits-all treatment plan.

Dr. Nguyen has practiced in San Jose for more than 20 years. When appropriate, care can combine chiropractic treatment with Active Release Technique, Graston Technique, posture guidance and corrective exercise.

Many people accept neck pain as normal — especially if they sit at a desk all day. But chronic tension and restricted movement often have addressable causes that chiropractic care may help with.

Common Causes of Neck Pain

  • Forward head posture — prolonged screen or desk positions can increase demand on the neck and upper back
  • Upper cross syndrome — tight chest and neck muscles combined with weak upper back muscles
  • Whiplash — post-accident cervical injury affecting muscles, ligaments, and joints
  • Cervical spinal dysfunction — restricted segments affecting movement and potentially irritating nerves
  • Muscle tension — chronic tightness in the trapezius, levator scapulae, and suboccipital muscles
  • Disc issues — cervical disc irritation or herniation causing local or radiating symptoms

How Chiropractic Care May Help

Dr. Nguyen evaluates the cervical spine, upper thoracic spine, and surrounding musculature to identify the specific contributors to your neck pain. Care may include cervical adjustments, targeted soft-tissue therapy, and guidance on posture and ergonomics. Individual results vary, and Dr. Nguyen will be realistic with you about what care can address for your specific situation.

What to Expect at Your Neck Pain Evaluation

  1. Explain what you are feeling — discuss when symptoms began, what changes them, and which activities are limited.
  2. Evaluate movement and function — assess neck and upper-back mobility, posture, soft tissue and relevant orthopedic or neurological findings.
  3. Understand the findings — receive a clear explanation of whether chiropractic care may be appropriate and what the next step should be.

Common Questions

Neck Pain FAQs

Can chiropractic help with chronic neck pain?

Chiropractic care may address joint, soft-tissue, posture and movement factors contributing to neck pain. Dr. Nguyen evaluates each patient before discussing whether care may be appropriate. Individual results vary.

What happens at the first neck pain visit?

Dr. Nguyen reviews your symptoms and goals, evaluates posture, neck and upper-back movement, and relevant orthopedic or neurological findings, then explains the findings and appropriate next steps.

Do you evaluate tech neck and forward head posture?

Yes. The evaluation can include neck and upper-back mobility, posture, screen and desk habits, and related muscle imbalances. Recommendations are based on the individual examination.

What is upper cross syndrome?

Upper cross syndrome is a postural pattern involving tight chest and neck muscles combined with weak upper back muscles, often contributing to forward head posture, rounded shoulders, and neck pain.

Can chiropractic help with tension headaches?

Headaches associated with neck tension or cervical joint dysfunction may respond to appropriate care. Dr. Nguyen first evaluates whether the symptoms appear musculoskeletal and whether referral is needed. Results vary by individual.

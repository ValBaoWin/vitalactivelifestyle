Our Care Priorities

Mobility First.
Strength Second.

Our first goal is to restore comfortable, functional mobility. Once you can move through an appropriate range of motion, we build the strength and stability needed to support it.

Strengthening a restricted movement pattern may reinforce compensations and place unnecessary stress on other areas of the body. That’s why we improve movement quality first, then progressively strengthen it.

Treat the cause, not just the symptoms.

We look beyond where it hurts to identify the joint, soft-tissue, posture, and movement factors that may be contributing to the problem.

Meet Your Doctor

Dr. Lam Nguyen, D.C.

Doctor of Chiropractic · VAL Chiropractic, San Jose, CA

Dr. Lam Nguyen founded VAL Chiropractic with a clear mission: deliver honest, effective chiropractic care that makes a genuine difference in patients' lives. A San Jose community member, Dr. Nguyen graduated from Life Chiropractic College West in 2004 and has been serving the Evergreen and East San Jose communities ever since — opening VAL Chiropractic in 2010.

With over 20 years of clinical experience, Dr. Nguyen focuses on soft-tissue injuries, sports rehabilitation, and auto accident recovery. He is certified in Active Release Technique (ART) and the Graston Technique—manual therapies used as part of an individualized plan. He is also a California Qualified Medical Examiner (QME) in the workers’ compensation system.

“Pain is a signal, not a sentence.”

Whether you’re a competitive athlete, a desk worker, or recovering from a car accident, the goal is to relieve your pain, help you understand why it happened, and give you the tools to keep it from returning.

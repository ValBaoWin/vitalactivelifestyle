Personal Injury Chiropractic Care in San Jose

Accident injuries do not always feel their worst immediately. Neck pain, back pain, headaches, stiffness, or reduced movement may become more noticeable after the initial stress response settles. Dr. Nguyen reviews how the injury happened, what activities now feel different, and whether chiropractic care is appropriate.

Concerns Commonly Evaluated After an Accident

  • Whiplash, neck stiffness, or pain when turning the head
  • Upper- or lower-back pain with sitting, standing, bending, or lifting
  • Headaches associated with neck tension or restricted movement
  • Shoulder, chest, or upper-body discomfort after seatbelt loading
  • Soft-tissue tenderness, muscle guarding, or reduced activity tolerance
  • Difficulty returning to work, exercise, sleep, or normal daily movement

Seek urgent care when appropriate: Loss of consciousness, chest pain, severe or worsening headache, new weakness, numbness, confusion, or difficulty walking requires immediate medical attention.

Referrals and Additional Testing

Chiropractic care is not appropriate for every injury. When the history or examination indicates that more information or another type of care may be needed, Dr. Nguyen can refer you for X-rays, MRI, pain management, or consultation with another medical provider. Imaging and specialist referrals are based on clinical findings rather than ordered automatically for every case.

What Happens Next

  1. Start with a free consultation — tell us what happened, what has changed, and whether you already have a claim or attorney.
  2. Complete an examination — Dr. Nguyen evaluates painful and restricted movement and determines whether imaging or another medical referral is appropriate.
  3. Receive clear recommendations — understand the findings, appropriate care options, documentation, and available payment arrangements.

Working With a Personal Injury Attorney

If you are represented by a personal injury attorney, VAL Chiropractic can coordinate with the attorney’s office when you authorize us to do so. The office can provide requested clinical records, treatment notes, visit dates, progress documentation, and billing information related to your care.

Clear roles throughout your case: Dr. Nguyen remains focused on your examination, treatment recommendations, referrals, and clinical progress. Your attorney handles legal advice, insurance negotiations, and decisions about your injury claim.

Understanding Lien-Based Care

A medical lien is a written payment arrangement that may allow qualifying patients to begin accepted care without paying the full balance at each visit. Payment may be deferred and handled from a future settlement. Acceptance is not automatic, and all terms are reviewed before care begins.

A lien does not guarantee legal recovery or a particular health outcome. Questions about legal rights, liability, or settlement decisions should be directed to a qualified attorney.

What to Bring to Your Consultation

Bring photo identification and any available medical records, imaging reports, claim information, or attorney contact information. You can still call if you do not yet have every document; the office will explain what is needed next.

Common Questions

Personal Injury FAQs

What does a personal injury chiropractor do?

A personal injury chiropractor evaluates musculoskeletal concerns after an accident, recommends care based on clinical findings, documents visits and progress, and can coordinate authorized record or billing requests. VAL Chiropractic does not provide legal advice.

What is lien-based chiropractic care?

For qualifying personal injury cases, a lien may allow payment for accepted care to be deferred and handled from a future settlement. Eligibility and terms are reviewed before care begins.

Do I need an attorney before contacting VAL Chiropractic?

No. You may contact the office first for a clinical consultation and an explanation of available administrative options. VAL Chiropractic does not provide legal advice.

Can VAL Chiropractic refer me for X-rays, MRI, or pain management?

When the history or examination indicates that additional testing or another type of care may be appropriate, Dr. Nguyen can refer you for diagnostic imaging, pain management, or consultation with another medical provider.

What records are maintained for a personal injury case?

The office maintains clinical records such as the patient history, examination findings, care plan, visit dates, and documented progress. Records are shared only with the patient's authorization or as otherwise permitted by law.

What should I bring to a personal injury consultation?

Bring photo identification, any available medical or imaging reports, claim information, and attorney contact information if you are represented. Do not delay calling if you do not yet have every document.

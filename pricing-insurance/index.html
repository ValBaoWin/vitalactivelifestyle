Common questions

Pricing & Insurance FAQs

Do I need insurance to receive care?⌄

No. The cash rate is $210 for a first visit and $150 for a follow-up visit. A 10-visit package is also available upon request.

How long should I allow for my appointment?⌄

Please allow approximately one hour for your first visit, which includes the examination and treatment. Follow-up visits are approximately 30 minutes.

What is included in the gym session?⌄

The $185 individual gym session lasts approximately one hour and focuses on proper exercise technique, safer movement mechanics, and identifying poor form or compensations that may increase injury risk.

Can you verify my insurance benefits?⌄

Our office can help verify benefits before your first appointment. Benefit information is an estimate and is not a guarantee of coverage or payment.

Do you accept HSA or FSA cards?⌄

Yes. VAL Chiropractic accepts HSA and FSA cards for eligible services.

Are payment plans available?⌄

Please ask our office about currently available payment options.

How is car-accident treatment billed?⌄

Free consultations are available for motor vehicle accident and personal injury cases. The office will explain the billing options that may apply to your specific case.

Have a pricing or insurance question?

Call VAL Chiropractic before your visit. We will help you understand the information available and explain the next step.

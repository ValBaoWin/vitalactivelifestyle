Experience with purpose

Care that starts by listening.

Dr. Lam Nguyen graduated from Life Chiropractic College West in 2004 and has served San Jose patients for more than two decades. He founded VAL Chiropractic in 2010 to provide straightforward, individualized care for people recovering from injuries, managing persistent pain, or trying to move and perform better.

His work focuses on identifying the joint, soft-tissue, posture and movement factors that may be contributing to a problem. Recommendations are based on the examination, the patient’s goals and whether chiropractic care is appropriate.

“Pain is a signal, not a sentence.”

The goal is to relieve pain, help patients understand why it happened and give them practical tools to help keep it from returning.

Clinical approach

Mobility first.
Strength second.

Restricted movement can lead the body to compensate. Dr. Nguyen first works to improve comfortable, functional mobility, then builds the strength and stability needed to support that movement.

01
Listen and examine

Understand the history, symptoms, goals and relevant clinical findings.

02
Explain the findings

Make the problem and the recommended next step clear before care proceeds.

03
Restore movement

Use appropriate joint and soft-tissue care to address restricted or uncomfortable movement.

04
Build resilience

Add corrective exercise, technique coaching and progressive strength when appropriate.

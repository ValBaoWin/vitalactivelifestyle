What the Free Consultation Includes

  • A conversation about the collision or incident and when it occurred
  • A chance to describe your current symptoms and concerns
  • Clear information about whether an in-person evaluation may be appropriate
  • An explanation of potential scheduling, documentation, insurance, or lien options
  • Time to ask questions before deciding whether to begin care

No pressure and no obligation: The consultation does not require you to schedule treatment or become a patient.

Comfortable, respectful communication

Speak With Us in the Language You Prefer

Feeling heard and understood matters—especially after an accident. Our team provides a welcoming, respectful environment and can assist patients in:

  • English
  • Español
  • Tiếng Việt
  • Tagalog

What the Consultation Does Not Include

The free consultation is an initial conversation. It is not a complete examination, diagnosis, imaging service, treatment visit, or legal consultation. If an examination or treatment is recommended, VAL Chiropractic will explain the next step and any applicable cost or billing arrangement before care begins.

Helpful Information to Have Available

  • The date and general details of the accident or incident
  • Where you feel discomfort and when symptoms began
  • Any claim number, insurance information, or available reports
  • Your attorney’s contact information, if you are represented

You do not need every document before calling, and you do not need an attorney to request a consultation.

When to Seek Emergency Care

Call 911 or seek immediate medical attention for severe or worsening symptoms, loss of consciousness, chest pain, difficulty breathing, major bleeding, new weakness or numbness, confusion, or any condition that may be an emergency.

Common Questions

Free Consultation FAQs

Is the consultation really free?⌄

Yes. It is a free, no-obligation consultation for all motor vehicle accident and personal injury cases.

Does it include examination or treatment?⌄

No. The free consultation is an initial conversation. An examination, imaging, or treatment is separate, and any applicable cost or billing arrangement will be explained before care begins.

Do I need an attorney before calling?⌄

No. You may call whether or not you are currently represented by an attorney.

What if I do not feel pain yet?⌄

Some collision-related symptoms can be delayed. We can discuss whether an in-person evaluation may be appropriate. Seek emergency care immediately for urgent or severe symptoms.

What languages are spoken?⌄

We can assist patients in English, Español, Tiếng Việt, and Tagalog.

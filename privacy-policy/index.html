Privacy Policy

Clear information about what this website collects and how it is used.

Home › Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Information you choose to provide

The appointment request form asks for basic contact information, the general type of request, and how and when you prefer to be contacted. Please do not submit medical histories, diagnoses, insurance identifiers, Social Security numbers, or other sensitive medical or financial information through this website.

How form information is used

VAL Chiropractic uses the information you submit to respond to your appointment request or general question. The form is processed by Formspree, a third-party form delivery service, so the information can be delivered to the office. Website form submissions are not intended for emergency communication.

Website analytics

This site uses Google Analytics to understand page visits and actions such as phone, text, email, and directions clicks. Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies and can collect information such as device type, browser, general location, referring page, and pages viewed. This data helps improve the website and measure outreach efforts.

Links to other websites

The site links to outside services such as Google, Yelp, Instagram, Injury and Wellness Chiropractic, maps, and Formspree. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

Your choices

You may contact the office directly instead of using the website form. Call (408) 532-1139, text (408) 386-6670, or email lamnguyendc@gmail.com. You can also limit or block cookies through your browser settings.

Questions

For questions about this policy or a website submission, contact VAL Chiropractic at 3239 South White Road, San Jose, CA 95148 or call (408) 532-1139.
